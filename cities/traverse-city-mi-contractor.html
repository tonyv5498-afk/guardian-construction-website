Common Questions

FAQs — Traverse City ROOFING

🏠 Roofing
What roofing system does Guardian Construction recommend for Traverse City? +
For Traverse City's Northern Michigan climate, we recommend architectural shingles with full ice-and-water shield underlayment as a baseline, with metal roofing as a premium option for maximum longevity. Snow loads, freeze-thaw cycles, and moisture exposure all factor into our specification — we design roofing systems for performance, not just appearance.
How do you prevent ice dams on Traverse City area homes? +
Ice dams are a building performance problem, not just a roofing problem. The real solution is proper attic air sealing and insulation that keeps heat inside the living space rather than escaping through the ceiling and melting snow on the roof. We address the cause with building science, then back it up with quality ice-and-water shield during roofing.
🏡 Siding
What siding holds up best in Traverse City's climate? +
Fiber cement siding (James Hardie) is our top recommendation for Traverse City — it outperforms vinyl in extreme cold, resists moisture and freeze-thaw damage, and holds paint significantly longer. It's the most durable option for Northern Michigan's demanding conditions. Premium vinyl is a solid more affordable alternative.
How does siding replacement improve building performance in Traverse City? +
New siding combined with continuous insulation sheathing beneath it is one of the highest-impact building envelope improvements possible. Adding 1" of rigid foam under new siding can eliminate thermal bridging through studs — reducing heat loss through walls by 30% or more, which matters enormously in Traverse City's heating season.
🔋 Insulation
What insulation R-value does a Traverse City home need? +
Northern Michigan's climate zone calls for R-60 in attics and R-20+ in walls for optimal performance. Most existing homes in the Traverse City area fall significantly short of this, especially older construction. Upgrading insulation in this climate typically reduces heating costs by 20-35% and pays back within a few years.
What is a building performance assessment and do I need one for my Traverse City home? +
A building performance assessment evaluates your home's entire thermal envelope — where heat is escaping, where moisture is accumulating, and where the highest-return improvements are. We offer these as part of our project planning process. For Traverse City area homes, the combination of air sealing, insulation, and ventilation improvements we recommend based on this assessment can transform comfort and efficiency.
💧 Gutters
What gutter system works best on a Northern Michigan home in Traverse City? +
Oversized 6" seamless gutters with high-capacity downspouts handle Northern Michigan's heavy rainfall and snowmelt better than standard 5" systems. We always include leaf guards on Traverse City area homes — fall leaf drop in northern forests can overwhelm gutters quickly, and a clogged gutter in a Michigan winter creates serious ice problems.
How do gutters relate to building performance in Traverse City? +
Gutters are the first line of defense in moisture management. Water that isn't properly directed away from the foundation migrates into crawl spaces and basements, elevating humidity throughout the building and causing long-term structural damage. Properly sized, installed, and maintained gutters protect everything else we do.
🧱 Masonry
What masonry services do you offer in Traverse City? +
We provide chimney repair and rebuilding, tuckpointing, brick and block repairs, and foundation masonry work throughout the Traverse City area. Northern Michigan's freeze-thaw cycles are particularly hard on masonry — moisture enters cracks, freezes, and expands, accelerating deterioration. Tuckpointing before cracks become structural failures is always the right investment.
How urgent is chimney repair for a Traverse City area home? +
In Northern Michigan's climate, deteriorated chimney masonry can become a serious structural issue within a few seasons. Water infiltration through failed mortar joints freezes and expands, widening cracks and destabilizing the chimney. We recommend addressing any visible mortar deterioration immediately rather than waiting.
🪟 Windows & Doors
What window specification makes sense for Traverse City's climate? +
For Traverse City, we recommend triple-pane windows with low-e coatings and argon fill as the performance standard. The incremental cost over double-pane pays back quickly through reduced heating costs, and the comfort improvement — elimination of cold drafts and condensation — is immediately noticeable. We work with several manufacturers and will specify the best fit for your budget and goals.
How much can window replacement reduce energy costs in Traverse City? +
Replacing single-pane or old double-pane windows with modern high-performance units typically reduces heating costs by 10-15% in Traverse City's climate. The comfort improvement is often more immediately noticeable — eliminating cold drafts and radiant cold from window surfaces makes rooms feel dramatically warmer even at the same thermostat setting.
