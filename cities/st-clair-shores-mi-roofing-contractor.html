FAQs — St. Clair Shores ROOFING

How does St. Clair Shores' lake and canal proximity affect roofing? +
The moisture-rich environment near Lake St. Clair can promote algae and moss growth, and lake winds can stress shingle adhesion. We use algae-resistant shingles and wind-rated materials for all SCS projects.
Are there special considerations for roofing on canals in St. Clair Shores? +
Canal-side homes require careful debris management during roofing to prevent materials from entering waterways. We use containment systems and take extra care on all waterfront-adjacent projects.
What's the best roofing color for a St. Clair Shores home? +
Coastal blues, grays, and natural tones complement SCS homes beautifully. Charcoal and weathered wood are perennial favorites that pair well with the nautical community aesthetic.
