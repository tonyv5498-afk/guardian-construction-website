Remodels designed to prevent the next loss

Basements, kitchens and baths planned around where water gets in, so they hold up for decades.

Basements that stay dry

Drainage, a sump pump with battery backup and water sensors, planned before we frame.

Kitchens with leak protection

Leak sensors under the sink and dishwasher, and flooring that survives a spill.

Bathrooms sealed behind the tile

A waterproof membrane under every shower and shut-off valves you can reach.
