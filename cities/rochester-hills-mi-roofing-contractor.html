FAQs — Rochester Hills ROOFING

What's the typical timeline for roofing a Rochester Hills home? +
Average homes take 1–2 days. Larger homes or complex rooflines may take 2–3 days. We complete entire projects in one continuous stretch when possible so your home isn't left partially exposed.
Are impact-resistant shingles worth it in Rochester Hills? +
Yes, especially given Michigan's hail frequency. Class 4 impact-resistant shingles can reduce hail damage significantly and some homeowners see reduced home insurance premiums. We'll explain the cost-benefit clearly.
Do you pull permits in Rochester Hills for roofing? +
Yes, we obtain all required permits from the City of Rochester Hills before beginning work. This is included in our service at no extra charge to you.
Can you replace my roof in winter in Rochester Hills? +
Yes, we work year-round. Cold weather roofing requires proper techniques — we use manufacturer-approved methods for low-temperature installation and only proceed when conditions allow safe, quality work.
