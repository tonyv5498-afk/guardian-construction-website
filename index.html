How It Works

How We Work

No pressure, no surprises. Every project — remodel or insurance rebuild — follows the same thoughtful process.

01

Free Consultation

We assess your space, understand your vision, and flag any existing conditions — water damage history, foundation concerns, or drainage issues — before a single decision is made.

02

Protection Planning

For basement projects, we conduct a full investment protection review: drainage assessment, sump pump evaluation, water sensor recommendations, and a conversation about what your insurance actually covers.

03

Written Quote

Three detailed options with line-item pricing — including any recommended protective measures so you can make fully informed decisions. No vague estimates, no add-ons after work starts.

04

Expert Installation

Experienced crews who respect your home, maintain a clean site, and deliver craftsmanship built on both remodeling excellence and years of restoration work.

05

Walkthrough & Documentation

We walk the completed project with you, hand over warranty documents, and provide guidance on maintaining your investment — including insurance documentation when applicable.

06

Ongoing Support

Questions arise after a project closes. We remain accessible for warranty items, insurance questions, and referrals to trusted trades for anything outside our scope.
